I.
The Delta

Where the river meets the sea, the climate is already changing.

In the Sundarbans and the farm districts around Kolkata, families live with saltier water, stronger cyclones, longer heat and monsoons that no longer keep their calendar. They are the people with the most at stake and the fewest tools to plan ahead.

II. Field Notes

Climate is local, everywhere.

A flooded field in Kalinagar, a rainy street in Kolkata, a tree across a road after Cyclone Amphan. The same pressures show up at every scale: the farm, the city, the town budget.

Envira works at all three, because the decisions are connected even when the people making them are not.
