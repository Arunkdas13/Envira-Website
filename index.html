Why it matters

Climate change is not a distant problem.
It's a local one — everywhere.

Rural West Bengal

Farming families on the front line

Hotter summers, erratic monsoons and saltier water hit rice paddies and ponds first. The families who depend on them have the fewest tools to plan ahead.

South Asia

Finance that rarely reaches the ground

Climate funds tend to flow to large projects and capitals. Districts and villages need simple, credible frameworks to make their case and spend well.

Your community

Hard trade-offs at the town hall

Solar or a new pool? Fleet upgrades or a tax freeze? Local boards make climate decisions every budget cycle — usually without the data to compare them.
