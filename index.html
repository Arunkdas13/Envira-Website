Who We Are

Envira is a climate research and education initiative. Our research arm turns environmental science and climate economics into frameworks, dashboards and policy papers that governments, towns and institutions can use. Our education arm partners with schools in rural West Bengal to keep India's academic rigor and add the open-ended, creative research students rarely get to try.

The communities hit first by climate change are often the last to get the tools to respond. We think that should change, and that the students who live there should help change it.

Why it matters

Climate change is not a distant problem.
It is a local one, everywhere.

Rural West Bengal

Farming families on the front line

Hotter summers, erratic monsoons and saltier water hit rice paddies and ponds first. The families who depend on them have the fewest tools to plan ahead.

South Asia

Finance that rarely reaches the ground

Climate funds tend to flow to large projects and capitals. Districts and villages need simple, credible frameworks to make their case and spend well.

Your community

Hard trade-offs at the town hall

Solar or a new pool? Fleet upgrades or a tax freeze? Local boards make climate decisions every budget cycle, usually without the data to compare them.
