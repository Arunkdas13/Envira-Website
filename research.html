01 · Research & Policy

Research that ends up in a decision

Envira's research arm turns environmental science and climate economics into frameworks, models and dashboards that governments, towns and institutions can use, from a district in West Bengal to a town board in New York.

Our Approach

Most climate research ends in a PDF. Ours is built to end in a decision. Every project pairs a research question with something a decision-maker can open: a dashboard, a model, a scenario simulator or a policy paper written for the people who will act on it.

We work across three tracks: Local Climate Action Plans, climate finance for low- and middle-income countries, and emissions and carbon markets.

Local Climate Action Plans

Climate policy starts at the town hall

Most climate decisions are not made at summits. They are made in budget meetings. We build the analysis that lets local leaders see what each choice costs, saves and protects: where emissions and risks come from, which options exist, the 5 to 20 year fiscal impact of each, and dashboards residents can explore themselves.

Applied example · North Castle, New York

Sustainable Budget Strategy

Quantifies trade-offs across tax policy, fund-balance use and green investments.

Municipal Scenario Simulator

Models 5 to 20 year impacts of facility, tax-levy and energy-efficiency decisions.

Future-Ready North Castle

A roadmap showing how solar, EV fleets and building upgrades strengthen long-term fiscal health.
