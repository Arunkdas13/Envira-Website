Research that ends in a decision.

Most climate research ends in a PDF. Ours is built to end in a decision: every project pairs a research question with something a decision-maker can open and use.

Three research tracks
i.

Local Climate Action Plans

Emissions inventories, risk mapping and budget trade-offs, packaged so a town board or district office can compare options side by side.

ii.

Climate Finance for Low- and Middle-Income Countries

How international climate money could reach the places that need it most, with adaptation capacity and local ownership built in.

iii.

Emissions & Carbon Markets

Methane from landfills, carbon prices across markets, and the trade-offs between decarbonization pathways, analyzed with open data.

Applied: North Castle, New York

Climate policy is made in budget meetings.

We build the analysis that lets local leaders see what each choice costs, saves and protects over 5 to 20 years, then hand residents dashboards they can explore themselves.
