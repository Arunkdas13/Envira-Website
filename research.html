Local Climate Action Plans

Climate policy starts at the town hall

Most climate decisions aren't made at summits. They're made in budget meetings. We build the analysis that lets local leaders see what each choice costs, saves and protects.

  • Baseline: where a community's emissions and climate risks come from
  • Options: solar, building upgrades, EV fleets, water and heat resilience
  • Trade-offs: 5–20 year fiscal impact of each option on the local budget
  • Engagement: plain-language dashboards residents can explore themselves
Applied example · North Castle, NY

Three linked tools for one town

Sustainable Budget Strategy

Quantifies trade-offs across tax policy, fund-balance use and green investments.

Municipal Scenario Simulator

Models 5–20 year impacts of facility, tax-levy and energy-efficiency decisions.

Future-Ready North Castle

A roadmap showing how solar, EV fleets and building upgrades strengthen long-term fiscal health.

The same framework is built to travel — from a New York suburb to a district in West Bengal.
