Deep dive

The Kolkata Climate Action Plan

The Kolkata Municipal Corporation released the Kolkata Climate Action Plan (KCAP) for stakeholder consultation on World Environment Day, 5 June 2025, after work by an expert committee set up in 2023. The mayor called it "a Survival Action Plan for Kolkata". The commissioner described it as "a set of deadlines, obligations and guidelines".

Its priority actions include conserving the East Kolkata Wetlands, a city-wide Heat Action Plan, climate-smart interventions in slums, preventing biomass burning, expanding solar, and an accelerated phase-down of fossil fuels.

Sources: CANSA, June 2025 · Millennium Post

Timeline

How Indian climate policy got here

SCROLL SIDEWAYS →
2008
National Action Plan on Climate Change

India's national strategy. States are asked to write their own action plans to match it.

2012
West Bengal SAPCC

West Bengal's state plan: 9 priority sectors, 130 activities, 22 departments.

2013
Ahmedabad Heat Action Plan

South Asia's first. Later linked to about 1,190 avoided deaths a year.

2019
Delhi's first SAPCC

Delhi becomes the last state to submit its climate plan.

2022
Mumbai CAP · India's updated NDC

Mumbai sets net zero by 2050. India commits to 45% lower emissions intensity and 50% non-fossil power capacity by 2030.

2023
Chennai CAP

Chennai's first climate action plan, also targeting net zero by 2050.

2025
Kolkata CAP released

KMC releases KCAP for public consultation on 5 June.

2026
2035 NDC · Delhi SAPCC 2.0 · WB climate fund

India sets 2035 targets. Delhi submits its updated plan. West Bengal's budget proposes a Climate Resilient Fund.
