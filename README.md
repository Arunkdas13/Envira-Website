# Envira website

Upload everything in this folder to the root of your GitHub Pages branch.

## Pages
- index.html: home (drone dive, policy highlights, photo essay)
- policy.html: Climate Policy tracker. Interactive explorer covering India's NDC, the West Bengal and Delhi
  state plans, the Kolkata Climate Action Plan, Howrah (no city plan found), and the Mumbai, Chennai and
  Ahmedabad plans as comparisons. Also a net-zero chart, the Kolkata deep dive and a timeline.
  Each plan's facts and source links live in the PLANS list near the bottom of policy.html.
- research.html: research tracks and live tools
- education.html: West Bengal schools program
- join.html: ambassador application

## Settings
At the top of site.js: emails, social links, application link, and introYouTubeId (your own drone video).

## Photos
Real photos from Wikimedia Commons (CC-licensed or public domain), credited on the page and in the footer.
They load straight from Commons. The list is PHOTOS in site.js.

## Keeping policy facts current
Plans change. KCAP is a consultation draft, and Delhi's SAPCC 2.0 was submitted in April 2026.
Update the PLANS entries when final versions are published.
